Arrival & Access

Getting in, parking, keys

›
Check-in 3:00 PM
Check-out 10:00 AM

⛓️ Driveway

There is a chain across the driveway. The padlock code is 1934.

Park anywhere in front of the house — just avoid the septic tank area.

Please avoid parking over this area

🚪 Back Door (Yale Lock)

You'll enter through the back door when you first arrive.

  1. Swipe your hand from the Yale logo down towards the door handle to wake the keypad
  2. Enter the last 6 digits of your phone number
  3. Press ✓ to unlock
  4. Lift the handle to activate multi-point locking.

Swipe hand from Yale logo towards handle to wake keypad

Your code is active for your stay dates only. Any trouble? Call Steve.

🔔 Alarm

The alarm pad is just inside the back door. Enter the last 4 digits of your phone number, then press Enter.

Alarm keypad just inside the back door

🔑 Keys (Pink & Black)

On the key hook just inside the back door you'll find:

  • Pink key — unlocks the two doors downstairs + the laundry
  • Black plastic key — unlocks the spa pool cover

Downstairs doors:

  • Left door → into the house (downstairs bedroom)
  • Right door → straight to the shower (perfect after the beach)

Keys on the surf wagon hook

🚿 Outdoor Shower

Perfect for rinsing off sand after the beach before heading inside (the septic tank doesn't like sand!). It's located just outside the downstairs shower entrance.

Outdoor shower for rinsing off after the beach

🏠 Layout Tip

Two bedrooms upstairs, one downstairs. Kitchen is upstairs. Bring your gear in through the back door and straight to the kitchen/bedrooms — the stairs are narrow and a bit steep, so it's easier than lugging bags up from below.

House Guide

Rules, tips & how things work

›

🏠 The Basics

  • No smoking or vaping inside the house
  • Please remove shoes at the door
  • Max occupancy: 8 guests
  • Quiet hours: 10pm – 7am

☕ Coffee Machine

DeLonghi Magnifica Start — it's a bean-to-cup auto machine in the pantry. Just press the button for your drink: Espresso, Coffee, Long, or Doppio+. Steam wand on the left for milk.

DeLonghi Magnifica Start — in the pantry

Mugs are in the drawer next to the fridge (below the bench).

Mugs & spices drawer — next to the fridge

🍳 Kitchen

Fully equipped kitchen with Miele oven and induction cooktop. You're welcome to help yourselves to any oils, herbs, and spices in the pantry.

Dish brushes and scourers are in the drawer directly under the sink.

Dishbrush and scourer under the sink

Cleaning products are in the corner cupboard to the left of the sink.

Cleaning products in the corner cupboard

🔥 BBQ

Gas BBQ (Weber Family Q with half grill and half plate) is on the deck. Cleaning brush, spatula, tongs, and BBQ cleaning wipes are hanging on the front. Please turn off the gas bottle when finished.

♨️ Spa Pool

Unlocked with the small black plastic key from the key hook.

⚠️ The spa pool is not fenced — it must be locked at ALL times when not in use. This is a requirement of the Building Act 2004.

  • Lock the cover immediately after each use
  • Shower before using the spa
  • No glass near the spa
  • Supervise children at all times

👕 Laundry

Accessed from the deck by the spa — unlocked with the pink key. Inside you'll find:

  • Washing machine and dryer (laundry powder goes in the left-hand side of the tray)
  • Second fridge with ice maker (cubes, shards, and chilled water). The freezer also produces big clear ice cubes
  • Outdoor furniture cushions (please bring out when using, store back when done)
  • Cleaning products, brooms, and mops
  • Cleaning wipes in the right-hand drawers

Outdoor cushions stored in the laundry

🚿 Water

The house is on tank water. Please be mindful of usage — shorter showers are appreciated, especially in summer.

🗑️ Rubbish & Recycling

Bins are at the front south end of the house, at the end of the lower deck:

  • Green bin: Recycling (large bottom, yellow lid)
  • Blue bin: General Rubbish (smaller, yellow lid)

Rubbish bags are in the drawer below the kitchen sink.

Collection day is Monday.

Rubbish bins at the front south end of the house

🧹 Before You Leave

  • Run the dishwasher if you've used dishes
  • Strip beds and leave linen in the laundry

    • Washing machine in the external laundry

  • Put all rubbish in the bins
  • Close all windows and lock all doors
  • Turn off lights and AC units
  • Run the Roborock vacuum (tap "Clean" on the dock or use the Controls tab)

Smart Home

AC, music, curtains, vacuum

›

❄️ Heat Pump / AC

There is one central anywAiR wall controller just outside the kitchen.

anywAiR wall controller by the kitchen

Please turn off when leaving the house.

🎵 Speakers

Bluesound multiroom speakers throughout the house. To get started, download the BluOS app on your phone — the speakers should appear automatically on the WiFi.

Once BluOS is running, you can often find the speakers directly within your **Spotify app** (via Spotify Connect) or use AirPlay.

📱 iOS App 🤖 Android

🪟 Curtains & Sheers

Motorized curtains and sheers — use the wall-mounted remotes in each room.

Top button = open, bottom = close, middle = stop/favourite position.

🗣️ Amazon Alexa

Alexa smart speakers are throughout the house and control many of the lights, curtains, and sheers.

Try these commands:

  • "Alexa, turn on Lounge Lamp"
  • "Alexa, open Master Curtain"
  • "Alexa, open Master Sheers"
  • "Alexa, set AC to 21 degrees" (if integrated)

Just say "Alexa" to get started.

🤖 Robot Vacuum

There's a Roborock vacuum that lives in the living area. You can run it anytime — just make sure the floor is clear of loose items first.

Press the button on top to start, or use the Roborock app if you'd like more control.

📹 Security Cameras

External cameras are active for property security. They cover the driveway and entrances only — no cameras inside the house.
