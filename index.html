House Guide

Rules and essentials

›

🏠 The Basics

  • No smoking inside the house
  • Please remove shoes at the door
  • Max occupancy: 8 guests
  • Quiet hours: 10pm – 7am

🗑️ Before You Leave

  • Put all rubbish in the bins by the garage
  • Run the dishwasher if you've used dishes
  • Strip beds and leave linen in the laundry
  • Close all windows and lock all doors
  • Turn off all lights and AC units

🔥 BBQ

Gas BBQ is on the deck. Please turn off the gas bottle when finished. Cleaning brushes are in the outdoor cupboard.

🚿 Water

The house is on tank water. Please be mindful of water usage — shorter showers are appreciated, especially in summer.

Smart Home

Control AC, music, curtains

›

❄️ Heat Pump / AC

Controlled via anywAiR. There are units in the living room and master bedroom.

Tip: Set to 21°C for comfortable sleeping. Please turn off when you leave.

🎵 Speakers

BluOS multiroom speakers throughout the house. Use the BluOS app on your phone to play music.

Download: search "BluOS" in App Store or Google Play. The speakers will appear on your local network.

🪟 Curtains & Sheers

Motorized curtains and sheers can be controlled with the wall-mounted remotes in each room.

⚠️ Beach Safety

Piha has strong rips and currents. Always swim between the flags when lifeguards are on duty. Never swim alone. If caught in a rip, swim sideways (parallel to the beach).
